Phy112 Lab3.2

In this lab you will examine many different types of converging and diverging lenses. First you will use the measure the focal length of flat converging and diverging lenses and implement the 3 principal rays with the LED light sources. Then you will use a converging lens to experimentally verify the thin lens equation and the magnification equation.
